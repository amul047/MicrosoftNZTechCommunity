# Microsoft New Zealand Technical Community

Microsoft MVPs organise events throughout the year to grow the community, network with peers, showcase the latest Microsoft updates, share customer stories and personal projects, and provide a safe platform for people to practice their public speaking skills.

Join the [Microsoft Tech Community](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/).

## 📞 Want to Start a New Group?

Interested in starting a new Microsoft technology user group in your area? Here's how to get started:

1. **Choose Your Focus** - Pick a specific Microsoft technology or platform
2. **Find Your Community** - Connect with local developers and IT professionals  
3. **Plan Your Format** - Decide on meeting frequency, format (in-person/hybrid/online)
4. **Get Support** - Reach out to existing organizers for advice and mentorship
5. **Register Your Group** - Set up on Meetup.com or similar platform

### 🤝 Contact Existing Organizers
Many of our current organizers are happy to share their experience and help new groups get started. Don't hesitate to reach out via LinkedIn!
